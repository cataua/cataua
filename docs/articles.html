The idea behind this section of the site is to share, with anyone interested, articles, projects, study notes, and even the points where I faced difficulties in understanding each topic.

In addition, I will share my experiences with electronics, programming, and other technology-related areas, aiming to be as thorough as possible and present everything in a clear, didactic way. Even so, none of the content here is meant to be absolute truth: the idea is for it to serve both as a starting point and as a space for reflection and discussion about the topics covered.
